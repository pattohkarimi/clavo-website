Electrical Works

We deliver safe, reliable, and high-performance electrical infrastructure for commercial, industrial, public, and infrastructure projects across Kenya.

What we deliver

Electrical systems designed for safety, continuity, and performance.

CLAVO Construction provides electrical installation and power infrastructure works for private developments, commercial facilities, public institutions, industrial sites, and civil infrastructure projects throughout Kenya.

Our work includes power distribution, lighting systems, cable routing, panel work, testing, and commissioning to ensure that systems are dependable from installation through day-to-day operation.

What happens

  1. Site assessment and project review
  2. Electrical design coordination and planning
  3. Installation of power and lighting systems
  4. Testing, earthing, and commissioning
  5. Final handover and maintenance support

Work in context

Safe, practical installations for modern working environments.

Electrical systems must be integrated carefully with site conditions, structural works, and operational requirements. We plan the installation sequence to maintain safety, reduce downtime, and support a smooth handover.
