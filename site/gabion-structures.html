Gabion Structures

Retaining and erosion-control solutions that help protect roads, sites, and communities.

What we deliver

Practical protection for challenging ground conditions.

We construct gabion walls, retaining structures, and erosion-control works for roads, drainage corridors, slopes, and developments.

Each installation responds to the site, water movement, ground conditions, and required service life so the structure supports lasting stability.

What happens

  1. Ground and drainage assessment
  2. Structure and material planning
  3. Foundation preparation
  4. Gabion or retaining installation
  5. Inspection and completion

Work in context

Protecting roads, slopes, and communities.

Gabion and retaining work must respond to water, ground pressure, and the surrounding road or site. We plan the installation around those conditions.

Key outcomes

Stronger slopes and protected infrastructure.

  • Erosion control
  • Slope stabilization
  • Road protection
  • Water-flow management
  • Durable stone-filled structures
  • Site-specific execution
