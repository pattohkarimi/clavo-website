Recent work

Five project stories from public infrastructure and private development.

Our portfolio reflects practical delivery across counties, estates, farms, and private properties in Kenya. Each project combines site planning, technical execution, equipment coordination, safety, and quality control around the client’s brief.

Public Infrastructure • Laikipia County

Street Lighting Across Laikipia West Wards

CLAVO delivered street and solar lighting works for Laikipia County Government across Laikipia West wards. The project included route assessment, pole positioning, foundations, high-mast and solar lighting installation, equipment access, electrical connections, testing, and commissioning.

By improving visibility around public routes, trading areas, institutions, and community spaces, the works support safer movement and more dependable night-time access across the wards.

Residential Building • Nyandarua County

General Building Works in Nyandarua

This Nyandarua project covered residential building works from foundation and masonry stages through external wall construction, finishes, and practical improvements to the completed home.

Our team coordinated materials, blockwork, workmanship, site sequencing, and quality checks to deliver a durable and functional building suited to the client’s needs and local conditions.

Drainage & Culverts • Rumuruti

Culvert Works for a Private Ranch

For a private ranch in Rumuruti, CLAVO carried out culvert and drainage works to improve water crossings, manage runoff, and protect internal access routes during seasonal rains.

The work involved setting out, excavation, formation preparation, concrete and channel construction, controlled water flow, and finishing around the ranch access network.

Roadworks • Private Estate & Coffee Farm

Tarmacking of a Private Estate and Coffee Farm

CLAVO delivered tarmacking works for a private estate and coffee farm, improving access for residents, visitors, farm operations, deliveries, and maintenance vehicles.

The project included surface preparation, grading, material placement, bitumen and asphalt works, edge coordination, roller compaction, and finishing for a smoother and more durable internal road network.

Gabions & Earthworks • Murang'a County

Gabions and Earthworks for a Private Property

This Murang’a private-property project combined earthworks with gabion construction to address slope stability, runoff, erosion, and protection of the developed site.

CLAVO coordinated excavation, ground preparation, drainage considerations, stone-filled gabion baskets, retaining alignment, and site finishing to create a practical response to the property’s terrain and water movement.
