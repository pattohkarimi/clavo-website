Civil Engineering & Road Construction

Infrastructure that connects communities, improves access, and supports long-term economic growth.

What we deliver

Roadworks planned for performance and built to last.

We undertake road construction and rehabilitation projects for county governments, institutions, private developments, and community infrastructure programmes.

Our work covers the full construction cycle, from setting out and earthworks through grading, gravelling, drainage coordination, pavement improvement, and final handover.

What happens

  1. Site assessment and setting out
  2. Earthworks, excavation, and formation
  3. Grading, gravelling, and compaction
  4. Drainage and pavement works
  5. Quality inspection and handover

From ground to finished road

Every construction stage is coordinated.

Good roads depend on the work beneath the surface as much as the final wearing course. Our teams coordinate the sequence so the formation, drainage, edges, and pavement work together.

Technical scope

Practical road construction for Kenyan conditions.

We plan around rainfall, soil conditions, traffic demands, access requirements, and the intended service life of the road. This supports reliable performance for urban streets, access roads, institutional sites, estates, and public infrastructure.

  • Site clearance and setting out
  • Cut-to-fill earthworks
  • Subgrade preparation
  • Grading and gravelling
  • Kerbs, culverts, and drainage
  • Compaction and pavement improvement
