Street Lighting

We install dependable street, solar, security, and high mast lighting systems that improve visibility, access, and public safety across Kenya.

What we deliver

Complete public lighting systems for Kenyan roads and communities.

CLAVO Construction provides street lighting and high mast installation for roads, estates, schools, markets, institutions, public facilities, and community spaces across Kenya. Our work covers pole installation, foundations, solar lighting, LED luminaires, cabling, electrical connections, and lighting controls.

We coordinate civil, electrical, and mechanical activities so every lighting point is installed securely, tested correctly, and ready for reliable operation in local site conditions.

What happens

  1. Site, route, and lighting needs assessment
  2. Lighting layout, pole, and foundation planning
  3. Solar, LED, high mast, and cable coordination
  4. Installation, connection, testing, and commissioning
  5. Handover, inspection, and maintenance support
