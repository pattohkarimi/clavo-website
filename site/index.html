Who we are

Built on integrity, driven by excellence.

Founded in 2025, CLAVO Construction Company Limited is a fully registered Kenyan construction and engineering firm committed to delivering innovative, reliable, and sustainable infrastructure solutions.

We combine technical precision with disciplined execution to create structures and spaces that are built to endure. From residential, commercial, and industrial developments to civil works and fit-out projects, our approach is guided by quality, safety, and long-term value.

Our multidisciplinary team of engineers, architects, quantity surveyors, and site professionals works collaboratively to turn vision into lasting reality—delivering projects that meet the highest standards from concept to completion.
